one project's code. This file records the notices those projects require. It
covers the artifacts as shipped; the licenses of this package's own source are

The solver itself. Neoklis Kyriazis (5B4AZ), <https://www.qsl.net/5b4az/>.

The corresponding source is the `third_party/nec2c` submodule, pinned to an
